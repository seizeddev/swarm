Do & Don't

Do

  • Parchment on dark, ink on light — that's the system.
  • Keep ≥ 1× cap-diameter of clear space around the mark.
  • Use the outlined wordmark SVG; pair body copy with Inter.
  • Let the mark breathe — it's the hero.

Don't

  • No gradients, glow, or shadow on the mark.
  • No rotation, skew, or stretch.
  • No accent tint — the brand is monochrome.
  • Don't put the bare mark on the squircle tile in running text (tile = icon only).
  • Don't re-typeset the wordmark in another font.
